## 2. EUROPEAN ACCESSIBILITY ACT (EAA) REASSESSMENT

Re-evaluated strictly under:
- **Directive (EU) 2019/882** of the European Parliament and of the Council;
- **Romanian Law 232/2022** (*Legea nr. 232/2022 privind cerințele de accesibilitate aplicabile produselor și serviciilor*);
- **Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC** concerning the definition of micro, small and medium-sized enterprises.

### Determination A: Is this individual developer's service an "economic activity"?
- **Statutory Grounding:** Under established Court of Justice of the European Union (CJEU) jurisprudence (Case C-41/90 *Höfner and Elser*, Case C-180/98 *Pavlov*), an "undertaking" or "enterprise" covers *any entity engaged in an economic activity, regardless of its legal status and the way in which it is financed*. An economic activity is defined as *any activity consisting in offering goods or services on a given market*.
- **Application to GetFlow:** While current revenue is 0, GetFlow is distributed publicly on Google Play with a configured in-app paywall presenting auto-renewing subscriptions and consumable credits. In EU competition and internal market law, placing a commercial offer on the market—even prior to achieving initial sales—is treated as entering the sphere of economic activity.
- **Conclusion:** GetFlow is treated as an economic activity for EU regulatory purposes.

### Determination B: Does the Free, Premium, and Flow Credits offering constitute an EAA-covered e-commerce service?
- **Statutory Grounding:** Directive (EU) 2019/882 Article 2(2)(f) and Romanian Law 232/2022 Art. 2(2)(f) include consumer "e-commerce services". Article 3(30) defines e-commerce services as *a service provided at a distance, through websites and mobile device-based services, by electronic means and at the individual request of a consumer, with a view to concluding a consumer contract*.
- **Application to GetFlow:** If GetFlow were a purely free utility with no purchase options, it would fall outside Article 3(30). However, because GetFlow presents interactive paywalls (`app/paywall.tsx`, `components/FlowCreditsModal.tsx`) enabling consumers to purchase Premium subscriptions or Flow Credits packs via Google Play, the application interface facilitates the conclusion of a consumer contract.
- **Conclusion:** The transactional offering qualifies functionally as an e-commerce service under Article 3(30).

### Determination C: Does a sole developer with 0 employees and 0 revenue benefit from the service-provider microenterprise exemption?
- **Statutory Grounding:** 
  1. **Directive (EU) 2019/882 Article 4(5)** and **Romanian Law 232/2022 Article 4(5)** unequivocally state:
     > *"Microenterprises providing services shall be exempt from complying with the accessibility requirements referred to in paragraph 2 of this Article and any obligation relating to the compliance with those requirements."*
  2. **Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC (Annex, Article 1)** explicitly defines an enterprise:
     > *"An enterprise is considered to be any entity engaged in an economic activity, irrespective of its legal form. This includes, in particular, self-employed persons and family businesses engaged in craft or other activities, and partnerships or associations regularly engaged in an economic activity."*
  3. **Microenterprise Thresholds (Recommendation 2003/361/EC, Article 2(3)):**
     - Headcount: Fewer than 10 persons (< 10 AWU); **AND**
     - Financial Ceiling: Annual turnover not exceeding EUR 2 million OR annual balance sheet total not exceeding EUR 2 million.
- **Application to GetFlow:**
  - Legal Form: Natural person / sole developer (explicitly covered by Recommendation 2003/361/EC Annex Art. 1).
  - Headcount: Exactly 0 employees (or 1 sole creator) < 10 threshold.
  - Turnover: Exactly 0.00 EUR <= 2,000,000.00 EUR threshold.
  - Balance Sheet: 0.00 EUR <= 2,000,000.00 EUR threshold.
- **Conclusion:** **THE SOLE DEVELOPER UNEQUIVOCALLY QUALIFIES FOR THE STATUTORY MICROENTERPRISE SERVICE EXEMPTION.** GetFlow is statutorily exempt from mandatory EAA compliance and from the administrative sanctions under Romanian Law 232/2022 (Arts. 17–20).

### Determination D: What evidence can a natural person without an SRL reasonably provide?
A natural person without a corporation cannot provide corporate registration certificates, REVISAL extracts, or corporate balance sheets. If queried by ANPC or any EU market surveillance authority, the following documentation is standard, lawful, and sufficient:
1. **Sworn Personal Declaration (*Declarație pe propria răspundere*):** Stating that GetFlow is developed and maintained solely by the individual, with 0 employees, and that annual turnover is below the EUR 2,000,000 threshold (supported by Recommendation 2003/361/EC).
2. **Google Play Console Developer Overview:** Confirming personal account status, registered developer name, and official contact email (`supportgetflow@gmail.com`).
3. **Google Payments / Play Console Financial Report:** Exported merchant financial statements showing exactly 0 transactions and 0 revenue.
4. **Personal Tax Filing (ANAF *Declarația Unică*):** Reflecting no commercial income from digital application sales.

### Determination E: Do disclosure obligations in Terms apply after considering the exemption?
- **Statutory Grounding:** Article 4(5) states that microenterprises providing services are exempt from the accessibility requirements *and any obligation relating to the compliance with those requirements*. Annex V disclosures (explaining how a service meets accessibility criteria in general terms) are an obligation relating to compliance.
- **Practical Recommendation:** While not legally enforceable against an exempt microenterprise, incorporating a voluntary, transparent accessibility clause in the Terms of Service is **STRONGLY RECOMMENDED**. ## 5. ROMANIAN TAX & COMMERCIAL STATUS (ANAF & LEGISLATION)

### 5.1 Current Status: Zero Revenue
- Under the Romanian Fiscal Code (*Legea nr. 227/2015 privind Codul fiscal*), tax liability is triggered exclusively by the realization of income (*realizarea de venituri*, Art. 58 & Art. 118).
- Because GetFlow has generated **zero revenue**, no income tax (*impozit pe venit*), no VAT (*TVA*), and no social security contributions (*CAS / CASS*) are due.
- Writing code, publishing a free software application, or testing in-app billing with zero revenue does **NOT** require registering an SRL or PFA.

### 5.2 Future Monetization Trigger (When Regular Sales or Ad Payouts Begin)
When the developer begins receiving regular disbursements from Google Play or AdMob:
1. **Continuity Principle:** Under Romanian commercial law, engaging in regular, recurring commercial activity (*activitate cu caracter de continuitate*) cannot be conducted indefinitely as an unregistered private individual.
2. **Lawful Legal Structures Available in Romania:**
   - **Option A: PFA (Persoană Fizică Autorizată) — Recommended for Sole Developer:**
     - Registration under CAEN 6201 (Custom software) or CAEN 5829 (Software publishing).
     - Tax regime: Real system (*sistem real*): 10% income tax on net profit + CAS (25% pension capped at 12 or 24 minimum wages) + CASS (10% health capped at 60 minimum wages).
     - Substantially lower administrative burden than an SRL; single-entry cash accounting (*contabilitate în partidă simplă*); no mandatory company employees; profits can be withdrawn immediately without dividend distributions.
   - **Option B: SRL (Societate cu Răspundere Limitată):**
     - Microenterprise regime (1% revenue tax) requires at least 1 full-time employee under a Romanian labor contract (*CIM*). Without an employee, an SRL is taxed at 16% corporate profit tax, plus 8% dividend tax and CASS upon distribution.
     - Substantial recurring overhead: Monthly accounting fees (*expert contabil autorizat CECCAR*), annual balance sheet filings, labor health and safety compliance (*SSM/PSI*).
   - **Option C: Copyright / Intellectual Property Royalties (*Drepturi de autor*):**
     - Governed by *Legea 8/1996* and *Codul Fiscal Art. 70–73*. Declared directly by the individual via *Declarația Unică* (10% tax on net income after 40% lump-sum deduction). However, ANAF often challenges app store sales as commercial activity rather than passive copyright royalties.
3. **Explicit Directive for Owner:**
   - **DO NOT register an SRL right now.** Incurring corporate setup fees and ongoing accounting costs while revenue is zero is fiscally unjustified.
   - When commercial transactions begin and Google prepares the first financial disbursement, the owner should consult a licensed Romanian tax advisor (*expert contabil CECCAR*) to determine the most advantageous structure (typically a PFA).
