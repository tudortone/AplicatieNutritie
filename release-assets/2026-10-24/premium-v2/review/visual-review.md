The main practical flaw found was undersized secondary poster text at phone scale; it was increased to42px (14px at360px display) with adjusted spacing and re-rendered. Another flaw was contact-sheet filenames clipped at the edges; labels were split into short two-line captions and checked again. All eight final English layout PNGs were opened individually in visual inspection; the complete180px and360px sheets and competing-direction sheet were rendered and examined. Video output itself was decoded, not inferred from source stills; actual frames were examined at full size and as a strip.

Titles remain deterministic editable text in the source, avoiding AI-generated lettering errors. One original food visual supplies a consistent restrained photographic motif; there is no synthetic device UI, extracted competitor artwork, inflated metric, review quote, external person's image or medical promise. Blank app regions are deliberately and prominently labelled; they are the unresolved source dependency, not an accepted empty final composition. Final UI readability, language accuracy and source/feature authenticity must be rechecked after binding actual captures.

This review does not assert improved conversion, parity with competitors' final screenshots or store readiness. The benchmarks set a production standard, but current lack of authentic GetFlow imagery remains decisive. No application functionality or native device behaviour was tested by inspecting these images.
